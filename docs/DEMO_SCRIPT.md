# 5–8 Minute Demonstration Script

1. **Context (30 sec):** “This is an academic DAA workbench. The two saved cases are illustrative; they are not official incident information or a dispatch service.”
2. **Overview (30 sec):** Point to the populated dashboard: two scenarios, eight locations, six scenario-specific stock records, saved algorithm results, and one measured benchmark.
3. **Scenario (45 sec):** Open `Assam Flood Response - Illustrative`. Review Guwahati, Barpeta, Morigaon, and Nagaon. Explain that demands and priorities are classroom values.
4. **Allocation (60 sec):** Open Allocation. Show the Fractional Knapsack and 0/1 Knapsack input differences; explain divisible supplies versus whole packages and compare with the available baseline.
5. **Network (45 sec):** Open Road Network and show the saved directed edges. Explain that reverse edges are separately stored even in this teaching graph and distances are configured costs.
6. **Shortest path (45 sec):** Open Route Optimization. Choose Guwahati Supply Depot to Nagaon Relief Hub. The saved Dijkstra example returns cost 120 units; describe this as the configured demo cost, not a road-distance claim.
7. **Tour (45 sec):** Open Delivery Tour and run the small four-node example. Branch and Bound returns the saved teaching tour; explain its lower bound and factorial worst case.
8. **Performance (35 sec):** Open Performance Lab and show the fixed-seed Merge Sort measurement (n=80, seed=42, five repeats). State that timings depend on the machine.
9. **Live India context (35 sec):** Search `Guwahati` under Live data · India, select the Assam result, and show current model-based weather. Remind the audience that NDMA SACHET and local authorities provide official alerts.
10. **Reports and close (35 sec):** Show Reports and the prepared eight-section report. Explain limitations: no verified needs, live inventory, road closures, or route navigation.

All illustrative values must be labelled and all displayed outputs must come from the running API. The report leaves student names/PRNs, guide and division blank for the team to complete.
